Crew

Take an oar

An open crew programme. Work on a part of one of our games, get your name in its credits, and if the game earns, share in it.

How it works

  1. Pick a game and a part of it you want to make better: a map, a season, a soundtrack, a language, a fighter, a village, the thing you noticed was wrong.
  2. Write to us. Tell us what you would do and show us anything you have made before, finished or not. We answer within a few days.
  3. We agree the piece in writing before you start: what it is, what done looks like, and what you get. Then you get the source, the tools, including the AI ones, and us on the other end of a chat.
  4. When your piece ships, your name goes in the credits of that game and on this page.
  5. If the game earns money, a share of it goes to the people who built it, in the proportion we agreed. If it earns nothing, we will have been honest about that from the first email. No one is paid up front. No one is asked to pay anything either.

Who we are looking for

Students, modders, artists, composers, voice actors, translators, historians, pilots, boxers, testers with a phone and an opinion. You do not need a portfolio. You need to care about one of these games enough to make one part of it better.
