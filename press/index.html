About the studio

Golden Harbour Games is an independent studio between two harbours: the Golden Horn in Vladivostok, where its founders grew up, and Sydney Harbour, where it makes games now. It makes small, sharp games with a real subject behind each one: wildfire aviation, boxing, the Russian serf estate, a used electric car across Siberia.

Every game plays free in the browser; the phone games are on Google Play. The studio writes and voices its games in English and Russian, and some in five more languages.
