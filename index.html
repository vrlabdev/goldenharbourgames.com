02 / What we care about

Games first

We were players long before we were a studio. Four things we hold ourselves to.

  1. We make games we love

    Every game here began as something one of us wanted to play and could not find. If we would not play it on our own phone on the train home, it does not ship.

  2. Good for everyone

    No manual, no account, no wall across the story. A child, a parent and someone with thirty years of strategy games behind them should all find their way in within a minute.

  3. We stay with our games

    A release is a start, not a finish. We try things, keep what works and change the games step by step. Write to us or post on the forum: we read everything, and what players tell us goes into the next build.

  4. We experiment

    VR, AR, a robot on the desk, a game inside a Reddit post. We try new hardware and odd ideas to find ways of playing that do not exist yet, and we show the results, good or not.
