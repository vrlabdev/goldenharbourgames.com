Experiments

This is the workshop. We use VR, AR and whatever else turns up to look for new ways to play. Each experiment has a write-up, pictures and a thread where you can tell us what you think.

Robots · Tools Eva and Adam, taken apart Two humanoid robots live in our workshop. We write the software that makes one of them see, listen and pull faces, and we built a 3D guide that takes both of them apart screw by screw. In progress Art direction One wheel, four tables In Souls & Estates one mouse wheel now takes you from a single peasant yard to a table map of central Russia, through four dioramas of the same place. You can try it New platform A game inside a Reddit post What if you did not have to leave the feed to play? We put Gull Chorus, our seagull flock game, inside a Reddit post: one post is one beach, with a leaderboard under it. In progress AR A lab in your pocket A web page that puts a relief model of the Earth, then our region, then our city on the desk in front of you, through your phone's camera. No app to install. You can try it VR Greybeard: a storm you stand in Five minutes on the deck of a ship off Cape Horn in April 1788, in a headset. A wave taller than the masts is coming, and you have two hands. Prototype VR A wildfire on your table AeroFire Command in a headset: the fire map becomes a table at waist height, the fleet is a shelf of miniatures in your left hand, and you give orders by putting an aircraft down where you want it.
