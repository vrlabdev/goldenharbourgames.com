Robots · Tools In progress

Eva and Adam, taken apart

Two humanoid robots live in our workshop. We write the software that makes one of them see, listen and pull faces, and we built a 3D guide that takes both of them apart screw by screw.

Eva
64 parts · 37 wires · 48 steps · 8 servos · about 3 kg
Adam
110 parts · 44 wires · 52 steps · 10 servos · about 10 kg
Built with
Three.js, the maker's CAD converted part by part
Languages
English and Russian

Art direction You can try it

One wheel, four tables

In Souls & Estates one mouse wheel now takes you from a single peasant yard to a table map of central Russia, through four dioramas of the same place.

Where
Souls & Estates, since build 0.100
Looked at
Tiny Glade and Foundation close in, tabletop strategy games far out, Dorfromantik for trees
Scale
1 unit = 36 m; it never changes
Cost
6 to 16 ms of GPU a frame at 1440p on a laptop RTX 4070

Rewiring Eva: a full software review

We took the software of a humanoid robot apart, reviewed every piece and rebuilt it as six small programs that each own one part of her. Then we gave her a new brain.

Robot
Eva, a desk-sized humanoid
Computer
Raspberry Pi 4, everything on board
Modules
Eyes, motors, voice, vision, cockpit, MCP
For AI agents
33 tools over MCP

AR You can try it

A lab in your pocket

A web page that puts a relief model of the Earth, then our region, then our city on the desk in front of you, through your phone's camera. No app to install.

Made for
The "Laboratory of Growth" teachers' forum, Norilsk
Works on
Android and iPhone, in the browser
Built with
model-viewer, three glTF models
Data
Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX (CC BY 4.0), Terrain Tiles relief, NASA Blue Marble

VR Prototype

A wildfire on your table

AeroFire Command in a headset: the fire map becomes a table at waist height, the fleet is a shelf of miniatures in your left hand, and you give orders by putting an aircraft down where you want it.

Game
AeroFire Command, the PC version
Headsets
SteamVR headsets, or a Quest linked to a PC
Table
2.4 m long at waist height
Measured
71 to 88 fps of 90 in the headset
