Gull Chorus

Runs right here in your browser. Nothing to install.

This game on itch.io ↗

Every seagull you send flies again with the next one. Lead the flock down a real beach, steal the chips, smash everything, and the whole beach plays the song.

Send a gull down the beach after a snack. Then send another, and the first one flies again right beside it, exactly as before. Then another. Soon you’re leading a flock of thirty gulls through umbrellas, kites, wheelie bins and very annoyed picnickers, and every bump, bounce and burp is a note of the song.

How it plays

  • Your gull chases a waving sandwich: point where to fly, down to dive and pick up speed, up to climb. Classic tap-to-flap controls are in the settings.
  • Skim a snack fast to snatch it in flight, or come in slow and land on the blanket.
  • Everything on the beach breaks. Knock a bin over and its food spills out. Bonk something sturdy and you bounce off a heart down.
  • Cross the finish line to end the beach for up to five stars, or keep going for more food, more smashing and more bubbles.

Six beaches, six songs

  • Bondi, Manly and Coogee in Sydney; Shamora, Glass Beach and Tokarevsky Lighthouse in Vladivostok, each with its own gulls and its own snacks.
  • Every beach plays a classic tune on real instruments: Waltzing Matilda at Bondi, the Wellerman at Manly, Kalinka at Shamora and more.
  • Finish a beach and the whole flock comes back as a gull tsunami that smashes the melody out of whatever is still standing.

Special gulls

Stars buy special gulls: Fatso with extra hearts, the Gobbler who swallows two snacks in one gulp, the Phantom who flies straight through things, Kaboom, the Seagull King and more. Each has a few goes per beach, so pick your moment.

Where to play

Free in your browser, here or on itch.io, and as a Windows download. Mouse, keyboard or touch: on a phone, drag to steer. In English.
