Stick vs. Weeds

Отп*зди траву

Runs right here in your browser. Nothing to install.

This game on itch.io ↗ Trailer ▶

Summer 1985. You are eight, you have a stick, and the nettles have it coming. A first-person slasher about a kid, a small Soviet town and everything that stings. In Russian, 18+, and very sweary.

Seryoga leaves the yard "not far" and keeps going. Between him and the river stands everything that stings, snags and gets in your face: nettles, burdock, thistle and, behind the boiler house, hogweed three metres tall. Draw a line with your finger and he swings from the shoulder.

He also never shuts up. He repeats everything he has heard from his dad, his grandad and uncle Tolya, and understands none of it: "Dad says they screw him at work. Strange. I thought he was a lathe operator."

How it plays

  • Draw a line across the screen and the stick follows it, like a bat from the right shoulder. A clean cut at the root pays double. A lazy one gets a remark.
  • Five days until sunset, with a boss at the end. Cottages, garages, a building site, the river. Each day is a different route through the village: a small map in the corner shows the way, and at the corners you choose where to turn.
  • Things you do not hit: rocks and rebar (sticks are not free), granny’s peonies and the neighbour’s garden gnome. Especially the gnome.
  • The neighbours. Grandad Matvey teaches the swing. Uncle Tolya bets you his front garden. Vitka swaps sticks for a pig in a poke. Aunt Zina pours kvass. The geese guard nothing; the geese are just like that.
  • Seryoga grows: respect, four stats and three skills, a two-handed spin among them. Sticks he finds go to the shed, and each morning he picks one.
  • Evenings: the day’s finds on the kitchen oilcloth, parents arguing next door, and a plan for tomorrow.
  • Two short modes: Zen on the wasteland, where nobody yells, and the Plot of the Day, one patch for everybody and one go that counts.

Before you press Play

The game is 18+. More than a thousand voiced lines, most of them rude, none of them meant for children. There is a switch that beeps the swearing.

It is in Russian only. The jokes are the game, and they are Russian jokes about a Russian childhood. If you would play an English version, tell us: that is how it gets made.

Where to play

Here in the browser, with a mouse or a finger, in a wide window or fullscreen; on a phone, turn it sideways. Better with sound. On Android the APK from the itch.io page runs noticeably smoother than the browser.

An early build: the year 1985 is in. The plan is for Seryoga to grow up through 1990, 1995 and 2000 in the same town, with different sticks and different problems. The voices, music, pictures and code were made with AI tools; the idea and the sense of humour were not.
