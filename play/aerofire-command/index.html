AeroFire Command

Runs right here in your browser. Nothing to install.

This game on itch.io ↗

Fight wildfires with air tankers, helicopters and ground crews. Read the wind, cut the line, and aim where the fire is going, not where it is.

A wildfire is running through the hills above a town, and you are the one on the radio. Everything you order takes time to get there, so every drop is a bet on where the front will be in thirty seconds.

The fire is a simulation, not a script

Every cell of the map has a fuel type, moisture and a slope. Fire spreads cell to cell, pushed by wind and terrain, throwing embers ahead of the front. Shift the wind and the whole thing turns and comes back at you. When a fire jumps a road, it is because it genuinely got across.

The coloured rings on the ground show how many seconds until the fire reaches each spot. Lay a retardant line and watch the rings bend back around it.

What you command

  • Helicopters from a light MD-500 on a bucket to heavy lifters, scooping water from the nearest lake or river.
  • Fixed-wing tankers, up to a 747 SuperTanker, reloading retardant at the airfield and laying it in lines.
  • Ground crews: smokejumpers dropped at the head of the fire, engine crews down the roads, dozers that cut a break where you draw one.
  • Water knocks fire down; retardant stops it coming back. Send two helicopters to one spot and they take turns on it.

Contracts

Six regions with their own fuels, terrain and weather: the Serra da Lousã, Los Monegros, Los Padres, Attica, Central Kalimantan and Central Siberia. Every map is generated, coastline, roads, villages and ignitions included, so no two runs are the same.

Pick a job on its difficulty and its fee, fly it, and get paid on what you saved. Buildings you lose come off the cheque. Spend the rest on aircraft, crews and the airfield.

How to play

Built for a phone held upright, one thumb, nothing to install. Drag a card from the strip at the bottom onto the map to send an aircraft; time slows while you aim. On a computer it plays in a tall frame with the mouse. English, Russian, Spanish and Indonesian.
