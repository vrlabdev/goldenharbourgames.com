Punch League

Runs right here in your browser. Nothing to install.

A boxing card battler. He shows you his next punch, and sometimes he's lying. Read him, build your combination, win three belts. Out now on Android.

Every exchange you lay up to three cards in a chain: a setup, a punch, a finish. Your opponent has already locked his combination, and you can see the first card he is showing. Is that jab honest, or is he loading a counter? Read him, then throw.

Plays on
Android · Browser · Phone
Languages
EN · RU

Combinations that mean something

  • A jab before a cross lands sharper. A body shot slows him down for the rest of the round. A block takes the initiative for your next punch.
  • Cards that work together light up in your hand, so you always know which combination is ready.
  • After the exchange the log shows what landed, what was blocked and who was first.

A real career

  • Three acts: the local circuit, the regional ranking and the national title, a belt at the end of each.
  • Rivals remember you, injuries need the physio, and every night between fights is a choice: train, rest, add a card, cut a weak one, hire a coach.
  • Twenty-one fighters with their own decks, habits and signature punches.
  • Your fighter levels up and earns talents; 75 cards to collect, upgrade and merge.

Where to play

The whole career is on Android, on Google Play. Here in the browser you can play the first act, the road to your first belt.

It plays landscape, so on a phone turn it sideways. No ads, no account. English and Russian.
